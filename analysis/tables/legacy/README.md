# Legacy tables — historical record, NOT current results

Everything here comes from the July 2026 laptop runs (`analysis/runlogs/`,
three seeds, pre-protocol configuration) or from single-machine sweeps of that
era. Every number was superseded by the ten-seed re-runs under the uniform
400-epoch protocol recorded in `results/results.csv`. Kept as provenance:

- `table_photo` — includes the withdrawn Photo 0.9089 draw (see REVIEWER_PROOFING §3).
- `table_yelp_multiseed`, `table_yelp_ablation`, `table_compression`, `runs` — three-seed lean-config sweeps.
- `sensitivity.csv` — three-run SimSample dose values, superseded by the `D_sim*` arms at n=5–10.
- `reproducibility.csv` — provenance of the original headline numbers, by hand.

Do not cite from this directory. Current tables are the siblings one level up
and `analysis/appendix.json`.
